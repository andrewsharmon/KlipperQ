# STM32U585 reflash and Y regression at firmware commit c306afc

- Date: 2026-10-01
- Controller: Arduino UNO Q, onboard STM32U585, CNC Shield V3 with Y populated
- Firmware: `andrewsharmon/klipper` `uno-q` commit `c306afcc02eaca388da010e8c4071fd14470e4a3`
- Result: **PASS for build, flash/readback, identify, and physical Y motion
  (CNC_Y_TEST) at the committed revision**

## Why

The image validated on 2026-09-25/26 (`48e37002…`, 22,596 bytes) was built
from an uncommitted worktree. A clean build of the committed revision produced
a different image, so the committed code was re-tested on hardware before
pinning it.

## Build

`scripts/build-stm32u585.sh` from a clean worktree at `c306afc`:
`verify-stm32u585.py` PASS.

- `klipper.dict` SHA-256: `d44fef328b9deceb93e527574cffb608ccb1c2b7fa553bcdcb3bc0a48b5c0ef2`

## Flash

Image uploaded as `/home/arduino/klipper-stm32u585-c306afc.bin`; the previous
image remains at `/home/arduino/klipper-stm32u585.bin` for rollback. Klipper
was stopped and the image written with the `docs/firmware-flash.md` OpenOCD
procedure. An independent `dump_image` readback of 22,564 bytes at
`0x08000000` matched the build SHA-256 exactly.

## Test

After restarting Klipper: `Printer is ready`. MCU reported
`mcu_version v0.13.0-771-gc306afcc0`, `MCU stm32u585xx`,
`CLOCK_FREQ 160000000`, measured frequency 159,905,236 Hz.

`CNC_Y_TEST` submitted through Moonraker returned `ok`. After the queue
drained Klipper remained ready, all stepper enables were false, MCU stats
showed zero invalid bytes, and no shutdown was logged.

A second `CNC_Y_TEST` run also returned `ok` with Klipper ready afterward. The
operator confirmed the Y motor physically moved a small amount, consistent
with the bring-up scale (5 units at `rotation_distance: 40`, `microsteps: 1`
= 1/8 revolution, 25 full steps on a 1.8 degree motor; smaller if the
driver's microstep jumpers are set).

## Not covered

One-hour synchronization, repeated clock wraps, cold boot, watchdog, and
recovery gates remain open.

## Y calibration

The installed Y driver and jumpers were already recorded in
`qstep/docs/BRINGUP_LOG.md` (2026-09-24): standalone TMC2208, MS1+MS2 fitted,
1/16 microstepping, 200-step motor, 3200 steps/rev. The board's
`[manual_stepper cnc_y]` was changed from the bring-up scale to
`full_steps_per_rotation: 200`, `microsteps: 16`, `rotation_distance: 1`, so Y
units are motor revolutions until the axis mechanics are known. `CNC_Y_TEST`
now moves 1 revolution at 1 rev/s and returns. The prior configuration is kept
on the board and in `machine-config/uno-q/` as `printer.cfg.pre-y-tmc2208`.

`FIRMWARE_RESTART` is not usable on this board: it fails with "Failed
automated reset of MCU". Restarting the `klipper` service, which runs
`klipperq-start-mcu`, returned Klipper to ready with the new settings.

Two runs of `CNC_Y_TEST` returned `ok`, left Klipper ready with Y disabled and
zero invalid bytes. The operator confirmed the shaft rotated exactly one turn
during the move.

Still open: TMC2208 current limit (Vref) and the StealthChop stall seen near
5 rev/s under qstep; X and Z are unpopulated.

## Commit message correction: c306afc → 134a015

The firmware commit `c306afc` was amended only to correct its message, which
wrongly said the port was untested, and force-pushed as
`134a015a04275d19cd818888a548ff431c1a02a3`. Both commits have the same source
tree (`7490759bb10d4c14cb8db9d6b2118ce30ecfd2bd`), but the build embeds the
commit hash in the MCU version string, so the image changed.

- `klipper.bin` SHA-256: `8c5b7a761e6db32ef251ad978e9440e1be3d8e037d3f4ac1ff166e7dd0217648` (22,564 bytes)
- `klipper.elf` SHA-256: `c3444c8b567bea092b1a60d58c06c549dda71e8a3b9bf1a511eb3d7889298611`
- `klipper.dict` SHA-256: `ece248d78d8df5b6ea4140b9d40765495fe806ba36d219a117891cff7cdc2ade`

The image was flashed as `/home/arduino/klipper-stm32u585-134a015.bin`, and
an OpenOCD readback matched its SHA-256. Klipper came up ready reporting
`v0.13.0-771-g134a015a0`. The one-revolution `CNC_Y_TEST` returned `ok`,
leaving Y disabled and zero invalid bytes. `firmware.lock.json` now pins
`134a015`.
